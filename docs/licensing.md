# Licensing

OpenTreasury is licensed under the **Apache License 2.0** — see the root [LICENSE](../LICENSE) file.

Contributions are accepted under the same license with a
[Developer Certificate of Origin](https://developercertificate.org/) sign-off
(`git commit -s`); see [CONTRIBUTING.md](../CONTRIBUTING.md).

Why Apache-2.0:

- Patent grant protects government adopters.
- Permissive enough for institutional integration work (connectors, forks for
  country-specific needs) without copyleft friction.
- Standard, well-understood terms for public-sector procurement review.
